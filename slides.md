# Slide plan

## 1. Keeping a Platform Alive — 0:00–0:45

Frame the operating loop: user signal becomes a visible decision and a validated delivery.

## 2. Feedback in Context — 0:45–1:45

Contrast the original in-product feedback idea with the current conversation-driven workflow.

## 3. Slack as the Feedback Surface — 1:45–2:45

Show Slack as the contextual entry point: a report, a structured bot request, and the resulting Linear issue.

## 4. What a Useful Report Contains — 2:45–3:30

Explain observation, context, impact, and evidence as the minimum useful signal.

## 5. Intake Automation — 3:30–4:30

Show the four automated steps: listen, triage for duplicates, structure or enrich, and create.

## 6. Two Flows, One Operating Principle — 4:30–5:30

Compare RedLens and Pedaxis: distinct channels, harnesses, and teams; the same connected loop.

## 7. The Thread Becomes Living Context — 5:30–6:30

Show that later messages enrich the same work item instead of creating fragmented tickets.

## 8. Linear Is the Decision Record — 6:30–7:30

Show assignee, scope, state, and evidence as the operational decision.

## 9. Triage Turns a Report into a Decision — 7:30–9:00

Triage collects the signal, assesses impact, and routes an actionable next step.

## 10. Estimation and Approval — 9:00–10:00

Estimate cost, effort, and risk; obtain approval; develop, review, and commit to delivery.

## 11. Before Shipping a Fix — 10:00–11:00

Reproduce, isolate, change, verify, review, and ship only after the QA evidence passes.

## 12. Closing the Loop — 11:00–12:00

Use the redacted closure mock to show the Developer's estimate and implementation update, Reviewer PASS, resolved Linear issue, and contextual reply.

## 13. Human Checkpoints — 12:00–13:00

Clarify the human decisions: prioritise, approve the right scope, and accept delivery evidence.

## 14. The Operating Loop — 13:00–15:00

Close with the reusable pattern: listen, decide, deliver, and return with evidence.
